Our Story

A long time ago in a college town not so far away...
A group of college students sought to better their BYUI community. They sought to fight against the injustice of lost items. This led to the creation of Rex and Found, a webpage devoted to helping you recover your lost items. Wether it be your phone, earbuds, or classified government projects, we're here to help.
